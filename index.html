The big idea

A useful test: does the technology follow fixed, pre-programmed rules, or does it learn patterns from data to make predictions or decisions?

  • Not AI: sensors, timers, and lookups that do the same thing every time.
  • AI: systems trained on large amounts of data that recognize, recommend, predict, or generate.
  • Maybe: many products come in basic and “smart” versions. Whether they count as AI depends on the model and features.
